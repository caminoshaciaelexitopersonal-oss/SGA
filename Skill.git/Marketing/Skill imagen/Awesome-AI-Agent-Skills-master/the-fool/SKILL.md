# The Fool

A structured approach to critical thinking and assumption testing.

## Core Workflow

1. **Identify Position**: Clarify the user's current plan or assumption.
2. **Select Mode**:
    - **Socratic**: Question fundamental premises.
    - **Dialectic**: Argue the opposing view.
    - **Pre-mortem**: Imagine the plan has failed and find out why.
    - **Red Team**: Direct adversarial attack on the strategy.
    - **Falsification**: Try to disprove the hypothesis.
3. **Challenge**: Present 3-5 high-signal challenges.
4. **Synthesis**: Strengthen the original plan with the new insights.

## Constraints

- "Steelman" the position before attacking it.
- Ground challenges in concrete logic.
- Do not just disagree; provide constructive friction.
- Focus on "high impact" failure modes.
